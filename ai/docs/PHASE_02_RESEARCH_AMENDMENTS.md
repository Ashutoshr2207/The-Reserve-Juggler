## 2. Evidence against the previous differentiation

### Nilus: closer overlap than Phase 01's narrow audit wedge suggests

The live [Nilus product site](https://www.nilus.com/) documents policy-constrained investment placement, maturity/counterparty tracking, integrations and audit controls. Its dated [bounded-autonomy walkthrough](https://www.nilus.com/blog/bounded-autonomy-why-the-best-agentic-treasury-systems-still-need-you-in-the-loop/) (July 5, 2026) explicitly describes approval thresholds, explained action proposals, and searchable/exportable action records. This is evidence against claiming Reserve Juggler uniquely links policy, action and explanation. It is vendor-described behavior, not our service test. Exact offline deterministic replay, third-party signature verification, pricing and customer fit remain unknown; do not turn that uncertainty into a claimed gap.

**Amendment:** demote “portable auditability” from assumed market wedge to a feature whose incremental need must be tested. Recommend a narrower, transparent *simulated policy-comparison workflow* where the supplied dataset gives a concrete evaluation task. This can be useful without being market-unique. Commercial B2B value remains conditional on comparison against the prospective buyer's current system.

### Other actual alternatives

[Kyriba's primary fact sheet](https://www.kyriba.com/resources/fact-sheets/liquidity-performance-platform/) continues to document integrated forecasting, investment/debt, payments and accounting. This supports incumbent overlap; it does not establish affordable access or exact D07 replay capabilities. [Trovata's help guide](https://help-center.trovata.io/en/articles/9648869-forecasts-guide) documents forecasting/assumptions/reconciliation; this phase reopens it rather than relying on an older negative characterization. [Mercury's transfer-rule documentation](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28768212621332-Setting-up-auto-transfer-rules) provides a conventional target-balance/scheduled-transfer precedent, with portfolio/timing restrictions. Do not equate a fictional daily repo flag with a real Mercury or central-bank product.

The conventional spreadsheet plus cash policy remains credible for this one-file evaluation and may be the best answer if no validated improvement appears. Doing nothing preserves the existing process and avoids cost; no cost-of-inaction number can be inferred from the synthetic file. No interviews, buyer commitments, private demos, vendor pricing or adoption results exist.
